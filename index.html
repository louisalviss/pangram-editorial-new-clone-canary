Elegant, refined and subtle.

A precise and elegant narrow serif designed for long-form copy with enough personality to make a statement as a title. In lighter weights it feels refined and restrained; in heavier weights its curves become expressive and striking.

The italic styles add a fluid, editorial character designed for pull quotes, captions and dramatic display moments.
