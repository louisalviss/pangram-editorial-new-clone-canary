Elegant, refined and subtle.

Designed for long-form reading with enough personality to work as a title, Editorial New balances classic publishing proportions with a contemporary edge.

Lighter weights feel quiet and polished; regular cuts keep dense layouts legible; heavier weights add contrast and confidence. The italic family introduces a more fluid, expressive rhythm.
